## Proposed visual derivation, ordered before choosing

The category rut is a centered marketing headline over equal download cards; its predictable opposite is an unreadable experimental terminal. Avoid both as the only choices.

1. Film contact-sheet publication: work-first selected frame, clear episode index, review/compare interaction. Familiar creator tool vocabulary; risk of becoming a video editor costume.
2. Independent design journal: large factual image plates and article-led reading rhythm, quiet metadata, precise index. Risk of making downloads secondary.
3. Color-coded specimen archive: category-color folios, rigorous specimen index and honest requirements. Risk of becoming a sterile documentation catalog.
4. Public workshop syllabus: skill sequences and example artifacts arranged by outcome, clear learning path. Risk of implying courses/progress that do not exist.
5. Media atlas: cobalt exhibition fields, white reading panels, yellow selection marks, an oversized real example surrounded by a compact skill index. Search/choose/understand requirements before download. Extends to non-video artifacts and articles without adding new visual systems.
6. Creative code exhibition: one interactive piece plus an expandable factual legend, selected artifacts grouped into an exhibit. Risk of GPU spectacle outweighing discovery.
7. Practical field notebook: demonstrations paired with annotated install/use instructions, structured marginal notes. Risk of nostalgia or decorative pseudo-handwriting.

Direction seed ran and assigned grounded candidate 5; reference `seed:ea15b162100e2d81`. This is a candidate for the user, not a recorded preference or design approval. The top-ranked film contact-sheet publication is offered as a full alternate. Each reference-world challenger is evaluated in the decision payload. No production UI is built before the user resolves the round.
