# Thai + English keyword captions — selected

User approved this treatment on 2026-10-29 in mall infection EP1 v2. Use it by default for new Thai-narrated JK Animation Studio videos, including Low-poly Urban, unless the user explicitly requests another treatment or no captions. This caption choice is independent of the visual style and Thai narrator voice.

Read [thai-en-keyword-highlight.json](thai-en-keyword-highlight.json) for reproducible values and inspect [the approved encoded frame](preview.jpg).

- Thai is primary: one short phrase in cream/white, dark outline, one meaningful highlighted word or phrase. Do not color the whole sentence or every word.
- English is a smaller secondary translation directly beneath Thai. Keep it concise and faithful; uppercase Latin. Do not add English narration.
- Amber highlights key information; coral marks threats/prohibitions; cyan distinguishes clues or the alternative route. Keep color use selective and suited to the scene.
- At 1080×1920 the approved sample uses Thai65px, English30px and a 5px dark Thai outline. Scale with resolution. Adapt placement around faces, props, signs and platform UI; do not reserve a blank subtitle band.
- Restrained140ms entrance and180ms keyword settle from1.06 scale; no decorative bounce, word explosions, or generic popup cards.
- Align cues with actual narration. Phrase estimates are not forced word alignment. Do not stretch speech to fit captions.

The earlier plain white Robot Terminal treatment remains historical example evidence; it is no longer the default for the next narrated clip. Existing approved video files are not retroactively edited. For HyperFrames markup use its captions guidance and the companion captions-overlay skill.
