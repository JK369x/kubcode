## Default approach

Use the built-in imagegen workflow to create a designed cover using actual episode frames as references for characters, locations and important props. Preserve the episode's recognizable visual language while improving composition, lighting and atmosphere. Code-based HyperFrames animation remains the video workflow; this cover preference does not request image-generated film scenes. Explicit new user instructions take precedence.

Choose the title and thumbnail promise together with yt-package. Use a short, clear main hook, one dominant emotional subject and strongly grouped supporting information. Place typography on intentional readable areas, away from faces, busy geometry and door/sign edges. Do not substitute a screenshot with text/number stickers for a finished cover. Keep the promise accurate to the episode; do not fabricate an outcome or guarantee clicks.

For interactive episodes, clearly invite the viewer to choose the next route in comments. Group the instruction with fully named numbered options and relate them to recognizable scene elements.
