# money-tip — เงินทองง่ายๆ (fact)

**เงินทองง่ายๆ · English: Money Tips** (key `money-tip`)

Formula: **ปัญหาเงิน → กฎ 1 ข้อ → ตัวอย่างตัวเลข**
Seen with visual style: `stickman-canvas` (any visual style is allowed). Legacy series name: `growkaz-stickman`.
(The two-minute-rule clip is a productivity tip told in the same shape.)

## Beats (≈ 34–37 s, 8 lines)
| # | beat | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook (0–2 s) | the problem as a number or question: "เงินหมื่นเดียว ทิ้งไว้สิบปี กลายเป็นเกือบสองหมื่นหก" |
| 2 | name the rule | title line ("ดอกเบี้ยทบต้น", "กฎสองนาที") |
| 3–5 | worked example | step by step with real arithmetic on screen (year 1, year 2 …) |
| 6 | contrast | the other side (simple vs compound; debt grows the same way) |
| 7 | why it works | one honest sentence, hedged |
| 8 | CTA | one action the viewer can take right now |

## Length / words
Real clips 34.3–37.4 s, ≈ 370–460 Thai characters.

## Facts
- Every figure is computed and the working written in SCRIPT.md (e.g. 10,000 × 1.1¹⁰ = 25,937).
- Name the source of a rule (David Allen, *Getting Things Done*; James Clear, *Atomic Habits*).
- Hedge outcomes ("มักจะ"); no promises of returns, no product/bank recommendation, no investment advice.

## Tone
Clear, upbeat, motivating; never shaming about money.

## Captions (content)
Amber = the rule and the numbers, coral = the trap (debt, procrastination), cyan = the alternative.

## Voice
Local JK `approved-calm-male-v1`. ElevenLabs only with the owner's own voice `jk` and spend approval.
