# ghost-story — เรื่องผี (fiction)

**เรื่องผี · English: Ghost Story** (key `ghost-story`)

Formula: **ฮุกเวลา/สถานที่ → เหตุแปลกซ้ำ → หักมุม → บอกว่าเป็นเรื่องแต่ง**
Seen with visual style: `night-vector` (any visual style is allowed). Legacy series name: `genhaha-horror-story`.

## Beats (≈ 35–42 s, 10–14 short lines)
| # | beat | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook (0–2 s) | one concrete odd fact with a time or place: "ผังชั้นสี่ ไม่มีห้อง 404" — no greeting |
| 2 | setting | first person (ฉัน/ผม), an ordinary Thai place: condo, dorm, last bus, night shift, antique shop |
| 3 | rule/normal | what is supposed to be true (the office says, the schedule says) |
| 4 | strange event ×2–3 | the same oddity repeats and gets closer; a time stamp (ตีสอง, ตีสามสิบเจ็ด) |
| 5 | silence | one real 1.5–1.8 s pause (bed dips, 1–3 soft SFX) before the climax line |
| 6 | turn | the rule flips back on the narrator ("ห้อง 404 อยู่ในผังแล้ว… แต่ห้อง 403 ของฉัน หายไป") |
| 7 | fiction label | spoken and subtitled: "เรื่องนี้เป็นเรื่องแต่งนะ" / THIS STORY IS FICTION |

## Length / words
Real clips 34.6–41.9 s, ≈ 310–350 Thai characters (pauses are part of the length). Numbers spoken digit by digit.

## Tone
Quiet dread, slow. **No gore, no jump scare, no faces of the ghost**, no screaming SFX. Fear comes from a rule
being broken.

## Fiction rules
- Always fiction; label it at the end (voice + caption). Invented generic places; no real building, brand,
  person or real tragedy.
- A *true* ghost/folk story would need sources and a different label; that is out of this format.

## Captions (content)
Amber for the odd number/time/place; coral only for the turn. Keep English line short and literal.

## Voice
Local JK `approved-calm-male-v1`, natural speed. The upstream kit's edge-tts default is not used. ElevenLabs only
with the owner's own voice `jk` and spend approval.
