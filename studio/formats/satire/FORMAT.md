# satire — เสียดสี (fiction)

**เสียดสี · English: Satire** (key `satire`)

Formula: **สมมติเกินจริงเกี่ยวกับคนดัง → บอกว่าเป็นเรื่องแต่ง**
Seen with visual style: `low-poly` (any visual style is allowed). Legacy series name: `satire-stories`.

## Beats (one clip so far: 73 s, 10 sections)
1. hook — the public figure's stated dream as a "ถ้า… เป็นจริง" question with the punchline built in
   ("แล้วใครจะมีเงินเรียกแท็กซี่ของเขา"); **fiction/satire label on screen from the first shot**
2. "สมมติว่า…" — morning in that future, everything looks great
3–8. escalating everyday gags, each with one twist (upgrade paywall, traffic jam moves to the sky,
   "free from work — and from salary", subscription popup)
9. turn — the real question under the joke
10. ending — loops back to the first image; open question

## Length
73 s measured. Shorter (35–45 s) versions should keep hook → 3 gags → turn.

## Fiction / fairness rules
- Label as fiction/satire on screen at the start (เรื่องสมมติ • เสียดสีอนาคต) and keep it clear at the end.
- Target ideas and public statements, not private life, body, family or protected traits.
- Never put invented words in a real person's mouth as a quote; never show fake product features as real
  (note in SCRIPT.md "not a real Tesla feature" etc.). No fake logos.

## Tone
Dry, playful, punching at the system, not at the viewer.
