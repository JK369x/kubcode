# celebrity-quote — คำพูดคนดัง (fact, real footage)

**คำพูดคนดัง · English: Celebrity Quote** (key `celebrity-quote`)

Formula: **คำพูดจริง 1 ประโยค → บริบท → ซับไทย**
Seen with visual style: `footage-reframe` (any visual style is allowed). Legacy series name: `celebrity-shorts`.

## Shape (≈ 21–54 s)
1. one real spoken answer from an interview/speech/press conference, cut so the meaning is preserved
2. context in title/description (event, date, why it is current)
3. Thai captions = faithful translation of what was actually said

## Rules
- **Never invent or paraphrase into a quote.** Do not splice sentences to change meaning.
- Record source URL, event and date in `SOURCE-AND-RIGHTS.md`; say when an older interview is reused, never
  present it as new.
- Rights: most sources are "all rights reserved"; credits do not establish permission. Note the risk; publishing
  needs the owner's explicit decision.
- Titles describe what the person said; no clickbait claims they did not make.

## Voice
Original speaker audio only (no synthetic voice over the person).

## Captions (content)
Faithful Thai, may highlight the key phrase; keep the speaker's hedges.
