# behind-the-city — เบื้องหลังเมือง (fact)

**เบื้องหลังเมือง · English: Behind the City** (key `behind-the-city`)

Formula: **สิ่งที่เห็นทุกวัน → เบื้องหลังจริง → ตัวเลข 1 ตัว**
Seen with visual style: `low-poly` (any visual style is allowed). Legacy series name: `low-poly-urban`.

## Beats (≈ 38–40 s, 8 lines)
| # | beat | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook | an everyday moment everyone knows ("ตั้งใจซื้อน้ำขวดเดียว แต่ออกมาพร้อมของเต็มถุง") |
| 2–6 | behind it | one mechanism per line, each a place/route/object you can show (route to the back fridge, end cap, eye level …) |
| — | the number | one real figure somewhere in the middle (time, distance, tonnes), sourced |
| 7 | fair turn | "ร้านไม่ได้หลอกเรา…" — explain, don't accuse |
| 8 | practical close | one thing the viewer can do next time |

## Length / words
Real clips 38.3–39.9 s, ≈ 400–460 Thai characters.

## Facts
- General practices are hedged (มัก / อาจ). Specific numbers need a source in "Fact basis".
- No store/operator brand named or depicted unless sourced and necessary. Say when web re-verification was not done.

## Tone
Calm, observant, "now you'll notice it everywhere". No conspiracy framing.

## Captions (content)
Amber = mechanism/number, coral = the cost to us, cyan = the reframe/what to do.

## Voice
Local JK `approved-calm-male-v1`. ElevenLabs only with the owner's own voice `jk` and spend approval.
