# night-vector — เวกเตอร์มืด หมอก แสงไฟดวงเดียว (Night Vector)

**เวกเตอร์มืด หมอก แสงไฟดวงเดียว · English: Night Vector** (key `night-vector`)

Look only. First paired with `formats/ghost-story`.
Kit: `styles/genhaha-horror-story/` is a **wrapper only** (STYLE.md + style.json). The original kit is kept
outside the repo (`~/Downloads/genhaha-horror-story`) and per project copies live in
`outputs/shorts-2026-11-01/genhaha-horror-story/*/` (`horror-kit.js`, `svg-kit.js`, `look.css`, `motion.js`).
Starter: `node scripts/genhaha-scaffold.mjs horror <slug>`. Status: **draft**.

## Look
- Vertical 9:16 dark flat-vector SVG scenes; fog layers; **one warm isolated lamp** as the key light.
- Ink `#05070c`/`#0b0f19`, night blues `#1a2236`/`#2a3242`, cream text `#e9e1d0`, lamp amber `#e8b66a`.
- Finish: slight desaturation, overlay grain (~0.22), blue soft-light tone `#1d2c4a`, strong vignette.
- People are silhouettes, mostly seen from behind; no faces on anything supernatural.
- Props carry the story: floor plans, door plates, clocks, CCTV frames.

## Lighting / camera
Slow purposeful dolly/push; lights die one by one; real held silences are also held in picture. No flashes,
no shake-scares.

## Renderer
HyperFrames (HTML/SVG + GSAP), Kanit font.
