# soft-2d — 2D นุ่ม ตัวละครหัวกลม (Soft 2D)

**2D นุ่ม ตัวละครหัวกลม · English: Soft 2D** (key `soft-2d`)

Look only. First paired with `formats/life-story`.
Kits: `styles/jk-soft-story/` (**selected v2, still visual direction only**, 2026-10-28) and
`styles/jk-soft-story-motion-v2/` (draft motion-beats pilot). Full look rules: `styles/jk-soft-story/STYLE.md`.

## Look
- Soft dimensional 2D vector: dark shaded round heads (charcoal/mauve lit side, blue-black shadow), two warm
  ivory eye shapes, curved Bézier limbs, readable hands, garments with a few purposeful folds.
- Trial palette: face `#1c202b–#50464a`, garment `#358487–#82c7bf`, warm practical `#f0c18e`, cool navy `#13263f`
  (starting values, not locked).
- Selective 1–3 px lit contour, no neon sticker outline; bloom only at motivated light sources.
- Editable rigs: `styles/jk-soft-story/components/hero.svg`, `radio.svg` (prefix ids per instance).

## Lighting / camera
Broad radial light on faces, directional gradients on cloth; environment → reaction → close contact with a prop →
explanatory comparison. Motion-v2 adds modest reframings timed to action.

## Renderer
HyperFrames (SVG + GSAP).

Our own art (reference video supplied by the owner used as private study only; screenshots are not assets).
